Clear terms and conditions

  1. Earn wallet credit equal to 10% of the full-price product subtotal of eligible purchases, excluding shipping.
  2. Credit is issued only after successful delivery is confirmed.
  3. Each credit expires 30 days after issuance. Spending does not extend its expiry.
  4. The wallet is personal and cannot be gifted, transferred or withdrawn as cash.
  5. Use wallet credit toward exactly one product unit per order, up to 100% of that unit's price.
  6. Any unused credit stays available until its original expiry.
  7. The credit expiring soonest is spent first.
  8. Wallet redemption cannot be combined with other discounts. An order using wallet credit earns no new wallet credit.
  9. Wallet credit cannot pay shipping fees.
  10. Returns and cancellations reverse related earned credit, with adjustments for any credit already spent.

Proposed rules for review only; not a live offer.

Examples

Spend EGP 1,000 at full price and earn EGP 100 after delivery.
